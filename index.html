How it decides

Four steps, every control cycle.

WELOS OS is deterministic: the same conditions always give the same decision, and safety always runs first. Pick a condition and follow one real decision through the engine.

See the full mode table
ConditionModeWhat changes
Emergency stop, battery, inverter or leak faultSafety isolationContactors open, turbine braked, pumps stopped, valves closed

Soiling at 18% with water, wind, sun and temperature in the safe envelopeCleaning panelsPump runs a 3-minute, 12 L rinse cycle
