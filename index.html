Power and water from your own roof.

W01–W03 Wind · optional module

Vertical-axis turbine

Catches wind from any direction and keeps generating after sunset.

Captures
Moving air
Becomes
AC power, rectified onto the 48 V DC bus
Controller
Brakes the rotor above 15 m/s or on any fault
Engineering detail

Fitted only where the wind at roof height averages at least 4.5 m/s, which in the UAE usually means coastal or high roofs. A three-month on-site measurement comes first.

Concept Noise, fatigue and measured site yield need prototype testing.

S01–S05 Solar canopy

The canopy does three jobs

It generates the power, shades the roof below and catches the rain.

Captures
Sunlight
Becomes
DC power through an MPPT charger
Controller
Compares output with a clean reference cell; cleans when dust costs 18%
Engineering detail

In UAE heat, panels lose output to temperature and dust. The sizing engine picks heat-tolerant modules above 25 °C and a cleaning method by local rainfall.

Concept Module count, wind loading and shading are site-engineered.

C01–C02 Enclosure

E01–E06 · C03 Power and control

One bus, one decision-maker

A protected 48 V DC bus joins sun, wind, battery, home and grid. The edge controller decides the routing on the roof, with no cloud connection needed.

Stores
LiFePO4 battery with its own BMS
Order
Home first, then battery, then grid export
Reserve
20% normally, 35% during storms and outages
Engineering detail

Hard-wired protection (breakers, surge protection, contactors) stays independent of the software. Emergency stop, battery, inverter and leak faults cannot be overridden from the app.

Working software The decision engine runs in Control OS and in the ESP32 reference firmware.

S03 · R01 Catchment

Gutter and first flush

Rain runs off the canopy into a perimeter gutter. The dirtiest first runoff is diverted away.

Rule

Engineering detail

UAE rain is rare but arrives in heavy bursts, so the gutter also manages storm runoff from the roof.

How it decides

Four steps, every control cycle.

WELOS OS is deterministic: the same conditions always give the same decision, and safety always runs first. Pick a condition and follow one real decision through the engine.
