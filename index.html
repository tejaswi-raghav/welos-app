ONE SYSTEM.
TWO ESSENTIAL RESOURCES.

Energy + water.

How energy moves

HARVEST.

Sun and wind feed one DC bus. Home loads come first; surplus charges the battery, then runs water treatment or exports to the grid.

The battery keeps a 20% reserve for outages.

How water moves

TREAT.

Rain becomes usable water in six steps.

  • Rain on canopy
  • Gutter + first flush
  • Storage
  • Filter + UV train
  • Pump
  • Output
The connection

NOT SEPARATE TECHNOLOGIES.

ONE INTEGRATED SYSTEM.

Solar power runs the pump and treatment. Stored water rinses the panels when the controller measures a soiling loss above 15%.
