01 Sunlight

The canopy is the power plant and the roof.

Six modules convert irradiance into DC power. A maximum-power-point tracker holds them at their best operating voltage and feeds the 48 V bus. The same glass shades the roof below and catches the rain.

    Clear-day simulation, 12:30. Values computed by the engine for the concept reference unit.

    02 Wind

    Wind takes over when the sun leaves.

    A helical vertical-axis rotor accepts wind from any direction with no yaw mechanism. Its direct-drive generator is rectified onto the same bus. Below 1.5 m/s or above 15 m/s, a fail-safe brake holds it still, independently of the cloud.

      High-wind simulation, 17:30. The turbine is an add-on, fitted only where a site measurement supports it.

      03 Rain

      Rare rain, never wasted.

      Rain runs off the canopy into a perimeter gutter. The first flush of each storm, carrying the dust, is diverted to drain. Only then does the storage valve open, through sediment, carbon and UV-C stages into an opaque tank.

        Rainfall simulation, 16:45. Non-potable use only; potable supply needs separate certification.
